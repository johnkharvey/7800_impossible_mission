## Yellow Room 1 - "Have a Ball"

![](../img/room_screens/yellow_room_1.png)

**Difficulty:** [1 / 5]

**Furniture items:** 6

**Enemies:** 1 Floating Orb (no robots)

**Strategy:** Don't linger when you enter this room!  It's one of 3 rooms without robotic sounds (the other 2 are code rooms), so you may let your guard down and get zapped right away if you aren't careful.  If you haven't properly planned, leave the room before the Floating Orb touches you and come back.

The best way to handle this room is to hug the perimeter.  The Floating Orb moves at approximately half the speed of the Agent.  Quickly run to the right, take the lift down 2 levels and then turn around and run left (not right!)  If done successfully, the Floating Orb will follow you back to the left without moving down a level.  Scan the furninture, and then move a little bit left to get the orb to follow you, and then repeat the same procedure: run right, take the lift down one level, and then run left.  This should allow you to clear the leff-hand side.

After that, run all the way to the right and clear out the both of the furniture pieces on the bottom-right of the level.  At this point, the Floating Orb will follow you down one level, but you'll still be safe.  Run to the right until the Floating Orb follows you to the right side of the screen.  Then, run left, take the lift up one level, and run right.  If done successfully, the Floating Orb will follow you back to the right without moving down a level.  Check both pieces of furniture, then run and hug the right side of the screen.  When the Floating Orb is touching the wall, it's time to make a break for it.  Run left, take the lift up 2 levels and you should zip past the Floating Orb.

At this point, you can simply leave the room.  But, if you want to have some fun, you CAN stand on the lift, wait for the Floating Orb to make its way underneath it, and then take the lift down all the way to the bottom.  This will push the Floating Orb offscreen, where it will suddenly appear at the top of the screen again.  You can repeat the process of trapping the Floating Orb down a level and then running past it as you did when solving the room.
