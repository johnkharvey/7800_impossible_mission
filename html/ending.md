## The Game End (SPOLIERS!!)

### After All 9 Puzzles are Solved

Each time you complete a puzzle piece, you reveal one letter of the nine-letter password required to access Elvin Atombender's Launch Room.

Here are some examples of passwords encountered in the game:

 ![](../img/ending/password_alligator.png)
 ![](../img/ending/password_butterfly.png)
 ![](../img/ending/password_crocodile.png)

Searching the ROM, we can see that there are a total of 8 passwords available:

 * SWORDFISH
 * ASPARAGUS
 * ARTICHOKE
 * CROCODILE
 * ALLIGATOR
 * ALBATROSS
 * BUTTERFLY
 * CORMORANT

Next, we need to get to Elvin's room:

 * [Spoiler](../img/ending/elvin_room.png)

Stand in front of the door and press "Up":

 * [Spoiler](../img/ending/elvin_final.png)

After just a couple of seconds, you'll see the "Mission Completed" screen:

 * [Spoiler](../img/ending/mission_completed.png)

And... that's it!  You have completed Impossible Mission!
